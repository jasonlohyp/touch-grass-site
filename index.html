Privacy policy

Touch Grass, Finally. Last updated: 15 October 2026.

Short version

There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name or email. The game saves your coins on your phone only. The game shows ads only when you choose to watch one. The game sends a few anonymous numbers so we can see if people enjoy it.

What stays on your phone

Your coins, grass, badges and settings. We do not send them anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a short ad to unlock a grass. Google AdMob shows the ad. AdMob may collect your device identifier, usage data and advertising data to serve and measure ads. We ask for non-personalized ads only. The ad rating is capped at teen. In the EU and UK, the game shows a consent dialog first. We do not use App Tracking Transparency, and we do not track you across other companies' apps. See Google's ad policy.

Anonymous analytics (Google Firebase)

The game sends four anonymous events on purpose: a day was played, a wonder was bought, a coin grass was bought, an ad grass was unlocked (with the grass name). Firebase also sends its own automatic events, such as first open and session start. These events are not linked to your name or identity. See Firebase privacy information.

Age

The game is for players aged 13 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

Your choices

You can skip all ads and still play. You can delete the app to remove the saved data on your phone. In the EU and UK, you can change your ad consent choice in the dialog.

Contact

Questions: touchgrassfinally@gmail.com, or see the support page.
